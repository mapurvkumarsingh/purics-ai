Security & Client Access

Your code stays yours

You retain ownership and control of your infrastructure. Purics engineers receive only the minimum access required to perform the agreed work — never shared credentials, never ownership transfer.

The Purics security promise

  • ✓ Your code, repos, cloud, and data stay in your accounts
  • ✓ You control repository, infrastructure, and production access
  • ✓ Engineers get only the permissions required for assigned work
  • ✓ Access can be revoked by you at any time
  • ✓ Activity stays auditable in your GitHub/GitLab and cloud logs
  • ✓ Secrets never via Slack, email, or documents

01

You own everything

GitHub/GitLab org, cloud accounts, databases, CI/CD, domains, secrets, and customer data remain yours. We never ask you to transfer ownership.

02

You invite the engineer

You invite a Purics-controlled identity to specific repos with the right permission level. Shared personal accounts are not how we work. You can revoke instantly.

03

No production by default

Normal path: development → staging → pull request → your review → production. Production access only when required — temporary, least privilege, logged, revocable.

04

Secrets stay in your vault

No AWS keys, DB passwords, Stripe/OpenAI secrets, or JWT keys through chat or docs. Prefer permission to use a service over exposing the underlying secret.

Standard secure onboarding

Exactly what happens to your code after you hire Purics — written as a process, not a slogan.

Step 1

Technical discovery

Architecture, repos, cloud, environments, and required work.

Step 2

Access plan

A precise list you approve — e.g. Repo X Write, Dev AWS limited, Staging DB, Production: none.

Step 3

Identity

Named Purics engineer identity — not a shared login — so your audit trail stays clear.

Step 4

You provision access

You grant the approved permissions in your systems.

Step 5

Work

Branches, PRs, and issues in your tooling — visible to you.

Step 6

Review

You keep control of merges and deployments where appropriate.

Step 7

Offboarding

When the engagement ends, access is revoked. Purics keeps an internal record of engineer identity, role, access granted, environments, and dates — so we scale without losing accountability.
